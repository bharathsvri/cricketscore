### Full Description
```text
B47 Cricket Score is a fast, reliable offline-first cricket scoring and match statistics application built for clubs, street tournaments, school leagues, and casual weekend matches.

Developed by B47 Tech, B47 Cricket Score combines a clean Material 3 interface with an accurate, battle-tested cricket scoring engine designed to make scoring matches quick, intuitive, and error-free.

🏏 KEY FEATURES

• Fast Live Scoring Pad
Tap large, responsive buttons to record 0 (Dot), 1, 2, 3, 4 (Four), and 6 (Six). Animated boundary highlights make live match tracking a joy.

• Instant Ball-by-Ball Undo
Made a scoring mistake? The complete state undo feature allows you to roll back any delivery, boundary, extra, wicket, or over change instantly with zero state corruption.

• Complete Extras & Free Hit Support
Full support for Wides (+ penalty and additional runs), No-Balls (+ runs off bat), Byes, and Leg Byes. Features automatic Free Hit delivery enforcement with on-screen visual banners following any No-Ball.

• Comprehensive Dismissal Types
Record all official cricket dismissals with fielder assistance:
- Bowled
- Caught (with fielder / catcher attribution)
- Run Out (striker or non-striker with fielder attribution)
- LBW
- Stumped (with wicketkeeper attribution)
- Hit Wicket
- Retired Hurt

• Match & Team Configuration
- Custom team names and editable player rosters.
- Custom overs (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, or custom over limits).
- Flexible player counts (from 2 up to 15 players per team).
- Ball type selection: Tennis or Leather ball.
- Toss recording: Toss winner and Bat/Bowl decisions saved permanently.

• Real-Time Run Rates & Target Tracking
- Big scoreboard display: Total runs, wickets, overs, and balls bowled.
- Current Run Rate (CRR) and Required Run Rate (RRR).
- Automatic second-innings target tracking, runs needed, and balls remaining.
- Automatic match result calculation (Win by runs, Win by wickets, or Tied match).

• Dual-Innings Detailed Scorecards
- Batting scorecard: Batter name, dismissal details, runs, balls faced, 4s, 6s, and strike rate.
- Bowling scorecard: Bowler name, overs, maidens, runs conceded, wickets, and economy rate.
- Fall of Wickets (FOW) chronological timeline.
- Complete extras breakdown (wides, no-balls, byes, leg byes).
- Share full match scorecards directly to WhatsApp, Telegram, or social apps.

• Professional PDF Scorecard Export & Sharing
- Export crisp, multi-page vector PDF scorecards with complete batting & bowling tables, fall of wickets, and result summary.
- Share match PDFs instantly to WhatsApp, Google Drive, Email, or save directly to your device.

• Full Database Backup & Restore
- Export a complete JSON backup of all your matches, teams, and player stats so your records are never lost.
- Seamlessly restore your match history on any Android device.

• Offline-First & Privacy Focused
Core cricket match scoring operates completely offline without an internet connection. No user registration, phone numbers, or account sign-ins are ever required. Match scores, teams, player rosters, and career statistics are stored locally on your device. Internet connectivity may be used for advertising and related Google Mobile Ads services.

Download B47 Cricket Score today and experience fast, professional cricket scoring on Android!
```
