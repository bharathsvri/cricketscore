2. Offline-First Architecture & Local Match Storage

B47 Cricket Score is designed with an offline-first foundation:

  • Local Device Database: All match data—including team names, player rosters, runs, wickets, deliveries, bowling statistics, extras, and tournament schedules—is stored exclusively on your device using Android Room (local SQLite database).
  • No User Accounts: The Application requires no signup, username, password, email address, phone number, or social media authentication.
  • No Cloud Synchronization for Cricket Data: We do not operate remote servers that collect, synchronize, or back up your cricket scores.
  • User-Controlled Backups: When you generate a scorecard export (PDF/CSV) or perform a database backup, the data is saved directly to your device or shared via Android's native system share sheet only to recipients you explicitly choose.

4. User Consent & Privacy Choices (GDPR & US State Laws)

In accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and applicable United States State Privacy Laws, the Application incorporates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Consent Gathering: On the first launch of the Application in regions requiring consent (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), a Google-certified consent dialog is presented to allow you to configure personalized or non-personalized ad preferences.
  • In-App Privacy Choices: You can review, update, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly inside the Application. Open Settings > Privacy Choices to reopen the consent settings dialog.
  • System Advertising ID Controls: You can reset or delete your device's Google Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:
    Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID.

5. Permissions Requested

The Application declares and uses only the minimum permissions necessary for advertising:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables Google AdMob to retrieve ad content and UMP consent configurations.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the Application and AdMob to detect whether an internet connection is available before requesting ads.

The Application does not request or access sensitive device permissions, including Camera, Microphone, Precise GPS Location, Contacts, Calendar, SMS, or Storage read/write permissions.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Cricket Records: Because all scoring records reside locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data. You can delete individual matches or player records inside the app, or delete all records permanently by clearing the app's storage via Settings > Apps > B47 Cricket Score > Storage > Clear Storage or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies. You can reset your Advertising ID via Android settings as described in Section 4.
